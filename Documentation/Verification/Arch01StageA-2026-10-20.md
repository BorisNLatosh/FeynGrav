## Changes

- New `Simplification/RestrictedCodec.wl` (codec: `failure`, `reject`, `encode`,
  `decode`, `decodeTree`) moved byte-identically from the donor adapter. It owns
  the shared failure helpers used by all restricted modules.
- `Simplification/Contractor.wl` now owns only executable discovery
  (`executableFileQ`, `findOnPath`, `resolveExecutable`) and the new
  `contractorPreparation` (resolve executable, build the Contractor command).
  Codec, job coordination, retention messages and the process runner moved out.
- New `Simplification/RestrictedCoordination.wl` owns `simplify`,
  `formPreparation`, `runJob` (moved byte-identically), retention messages,
  `$progressIntervalSeconds` and `reportProgress`. `simplify` keeps every
  validation and progress stage; the previous resolve/config and command-building
  branches became one `prepared` dispatch to `contractorPreparation` or
  `formPreparation`. Staging and decoding branches are unchanged.
- Loader: `CalcFormConverter.wl` now loads `RestrictedCodec.wl`, `Contractor.wl`,
  `FORM.wl`, `RestrictedCoordination.wl`, `Facade.wl` in that order; the
  per-attempt `Get` outcome list has five entries and the readiness inventory
  adds `encode`, `decode`, `resolveExecutable`, `contractorPreparation`,
  `formPreparation` and `runJob`. Marker, clearing, recovery and store
  preservation semantics are unchanged.
- Installer: dependency rows for `Simplification/RestrictedCodec.wl` and
  `Simplification/RestrictedCoordination.wl`; the Loading layout fixture now
  requires and stepwise-rejects all five facade companions in load order.
- `Simplification/README.md` module map updated (load order, ownership,
  consumed operations); `Facade.wl` is unchanged.
- Tests: `Reliability.wls` extends loader coverage to missing/repair of the new
  codec and coordination companions and adds a restricted algebra/policy parity
  section. `Loading.wls` loader and installer expectations now assert the
  definitions actually owned by Contractor.

Byte-parity checks: the codec body, Contractor discovery and `runJob` match the
accepted source exactly; `simplify` differs only by the `prepared` dispatch and
the removed duplicate command construction (diff retained).
