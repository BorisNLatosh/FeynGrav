## Implementation

- The polarisation accumulator combines structural layers in bounded buffers;
  inactive originals and the growing completed prefix are hidden during local
  sorts. Denominator-group visibility remains unchanged: an attempted broader
  isolation lost one QED literal-zero result and was withdrawn.
- The original master is scored once. A completed prefix can prove that the
  eventual candidate already loses the storage comparison. Distinct complete
  polarisation monomials cannot cancel across groups; reachable shared-block
  cost cannot decrease as further groups are added. The complete original is
  restored on this early rejection, with original forms still winning ties.
- A private, deterministic allowance of **1,024 rational passes per FORM job**
  bounds aggregate speculative polarisation work. Each attempted `cfcMRFinal`
  pass, including its bounded factor inspections and cache lookup, is charged.
  This allowance is distinct from the existing 1,024-structure-per-master limit.
  A denied pass changes no coefficient. Exhaustion restores the **complete
  original master**; incomplete group/denominator results are not cached, and
  subsequent masters retain their original shared form. Previously completed
  subproblem caches remain valid. Non-polarisation rational processing is unchanged.
- Emitted shared references receive dense labels, independent of speculative
  internal interning. Internal tables keep their IDs; only disposable output
  expressions are renamed. Definitions still precede their references. No
  public head, option, mapping version or mathematical capability was added.

The 1,000-term/12-variable/degree-24 rational selector and the
250-term/eight-variable/degree-12 factorisation selector are unchanged. These
are work-selection guards, not memory or runtime guarantees. Actual FORM errors,
timeouts and resource failures still propagate; none is silently retried.
Production does not call Wolfram `Simplify`, `Together` or `Factor`.

On this workload the first master's 138 structures were recognised. The job
allowance was reached after 20 groups, so its complete original was restored;
the remaining 16 masters skipped further polarisation simplification. This is a
general conservative fallback, not a special-case answer. The delivered result
is complete but is **not claimed to have maximally simplified coefficients**.
The small QED fixtures below complete their useful simplification within the allowance.

10% investigation threshold relative to the approximately 392 MB reference.
