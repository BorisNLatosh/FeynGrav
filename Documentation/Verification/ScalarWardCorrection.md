## Result

The canonical scalar action's full off-shell two-graviton vertex equals the
regenerated `Libs/GravitonScalarVertex_2`, separately for its kinetic and mass
terms, with symbolic D and unconstrained scalar momenta. The cubic vertex is
unchanged. Both minimal scalar source routines now restore the n! omitted when
turning averaged C-tensor coefficients into differentiated graviton vertices.

Each amplitude was generated by native FeynArts and converted with FeynCalc.
The tested contractions are

$$\mathcal A_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}p_3^\mu\xi^\nu e_4^\rho e_4^\sigma=0,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}e_3^\mu e_3^\nu p_4^\rho\xi^\sigma=0.$$

Here xi is arbitrary; the other graviton's auxiliary polarisation is null and
transverse to its own momentum. Crossed products e3.p4 and e4.p3 remain generic.
External legs are on shell with momentum conservation. D, scalar mass and the
additional couplings remain symbolic. No helicity table or D=4 substitution is
used. Symmetry of the graviton pair gives the corresponding symmetrised gauge
variation. These are the two Ward identities for this tree process, not a
claim about every amplitude or loops.

No diagnostic extra contact was added: the rebuilt native amplitude itself
vanishes under both contractions. Retained scripts inherit some historical
variable names (including OriginalWard and AddedContact); only the native
amplitude sums enter the successful tests. The separate g1-squared and g2
sectors pass in EsGB. The auxiliary EsGB coefficient assertions in the minimal
and John scripts are vacuous and are not additional evidence for those models.

## Regenerated interaction libraries

All 20 changed library files were inspected. Eighteen non-axion files passed
exact Wolfram algebra comparisons against their previous expressions with the
specified multiplicity corrections. In the massless vector/gluon expressions,
only the terms without differentiated gravitons double; connection terms do not.

Both axion files additionally replace legacy `LeviCivita[p1,p2,...]` text with
explicit D-dimensional momentum/index `Eps` slots. FeynCalc interprets that old
text as four Lorentz indices, so literal evaluated old/new equality fails.
After explicitly translating the intended legacy slots and respecting Pair
symmetry, exact algebra confirms unchanged order-one coefficients and doubled
order-two coefficients. This is a recorded notation migration, not equality
between distinct Lorentz spaces or validation of the unresolved axion action
convention. The first direct Wolfram comparison's two failures are retained.
